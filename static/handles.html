Usernames are now handles

What changed, and how to find yours.

What a handle is

A handle is your name on this site, written with an @ in front, like @jiayang. It is 3 to 16 characters and uses only letters, numbers and dashes. Capital letters do not matter: @Jiayang and @jiayang are the same handle.

You never have to type the @. It is only shown.

Signing in

Nothing changes. Your old username and password still work, typed exactly as before. Your handle works too.

What happened to my username

  • If it already fit the rules, it is your handle now, in lowercase.
  • If it had spaces or symbols, they were removed. You will see your new handle the next time you sign in.
  • If someone who joined before you had the same name, or yours used other alphabets, you are asked to choose a new handle when you sign in. Chat stays locked until you do.

Chat

Your chats, messages and friends are unchanged. To add someone, type their handle.
